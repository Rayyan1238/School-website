ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A Tradition of Education Since 1948

St. Lawrences Boys School

St. Lawrences Boys School is a boys' school located in Karachi. The school was established in 1948 and has been providing education to students for many years.

The school offers education from Nursery to Secondary level under the Sindh Board curriculum. The school focuses on good studies, discipline, knowledge and the overall development of students.

The school has two main buildings. One building is for Primary students and the other building is for Lower and Upper Secondary students.
